How to get your first orderon Fiverr in 2026.

The short answer: pick one narrow service, write a gig title with the words buyers search for, price your first package low, reply within an hour, and stay online daily.

1. Choose one narrow service

"Logo design" competes with thousands of sellers. "Minimal logo for coffee shops" competes with far fewer. A narrow gig is easier to rank and easier for buyers to trust.

2. Write a title buyers actually search for

Type your service into the Fiverr search bar and note the suggestions. Use those words in your title, tags and the first line of your description.

3. Price to get the first review

  • Make the basic package small and fast to deliver.
  • Raise prices after your first 3 to 5 five-star reviews.
  • Never offer to work outside Fiverr; it can get your account banned.

4. Reply fast and stay online

Response time affects how often your gig is shown. Keep the Fiverr app on your phone, reply within an hour and send a short, friendly first message that asks one clear question.

5. Follow a 30-day plan

Most beginners quit in the first two weeks. Treat the first month as practice: improve one part of your gig each week and track impressions, clicks and messages.

Quick answers

Common questions.

How long does it take to get the first order on Fiverr?

It varies by service and effort. Many beginners wait several weeks, which is why a narrow gig, fast replies and daily activity matter.

Can I get Fiverr orders with no experience?

Yes, if you choose a service you can deliver well, show samples you made yourself and price your first package to win early reviews.
