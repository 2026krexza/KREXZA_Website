In short

What is Freelancer OS? Freelancer OS is an offline business app for freelancers and solo service providers. It manages clients, moves proposals and projects through a pipeline, creates professional invoices saved as PDF, flags overdue invoices, tracks income and expenses to show real profit, and suggests a tax set-aside. Each invoice can use its own currency from 30+ options.

What you get

Everything inside Freelancer OS

  • Professional invoices saved as PDF
  • Client manager

  • Income and expense tracking
  • Overdue invoice alerts
  • Tax set-aside
  • 30+ currencies, chosen per invoice
  • CSV export and backups

How it works

Made for real days, not demos.

Freelancer OS keeps clients, projects and money together. Move proposals through your pipeline, create a professional invoice from a client in seconds, and save it as a PDF.

Mark invoices paid and log expenses to see your real profit and how much to set aside for tax. Overdue alerts show who still owes you, and each invoice can use its own currency.
