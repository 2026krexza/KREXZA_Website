Privacy policy.

KREXZA tools are private by design: no account, no cloud, and your data stays on your device.

The offline apps and templates

Student OS, Freelancer OS, Trainer OS and the AI Prompt Vault save everything you type in your own browser on your own device. The spreadsheet templates live in your own Google Drive or Excel file. KREXZA cannot see, collect or access this information.

This website

krexza.netlify.app does not have accounts, contact forms or advertising trackers. It remembers your light or dark theme choice in your browser. Like most websites, our host (Netlify) may keep basic technical logs, such as IP address and browser type, to keep the site secure and working.

The website loads fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when it sends the font files to your browser.

Purchases

Purchases happen on Whop. Whop collects your name, email and payment details to process your order and deliver your files, under Whop's own privacy policy. KREXZA receives the order details Whop shares with sellers, such as your name, email and what you bought, and uses them only to deliver your product and support you.

What we never do

  • We never sell your information.
  • We never see or store your card details.
  • We never send marketing emails without your permission.

Your choices

You can ask us what order information we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, by messaging KREXZA through the store on Whop.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
