Good to know.

Everything people ask before buying. Still unsure? Message KREXZA through the Whop store.

Is this a subscription?

No. Every paid KREXZA tool is a one-time purchase. You pay once on Whop and download it straight away.

How do I get my tool after buying?

Checkout happens on Whop. Right after payment, your files are ready to download from your Whop account. Most tools take about two minutes to set up, and each one comes with simple instructions.

Does it work on my iPhone?

The spreadsheet templates and PDF guides work on iPhone and iPad. The offline apps (Student OS, Freelancer OS, Trainer OS and the AI Prompt Vault app) need a computer or an Android phone with Chrome.

Where is my data stored?

On your own device. The apps save to your browser, and the templates live in your own Google Drive or Excel file. KREXZA never sees your data, and the apps include a backup button so you can move to another computer.

Do I need to know Excel or coding?

No. You type in the highlighted boxes and everything else calculates itself. The dashboard apps can load sample data so you can see how they work before adding your own.

Can I use these for my business?

Yes. Each license covers your own personal or business use. Please don't resell or share the files.

Which currencies are supported?

Every money tool works with any currency. Freelancer OS and Trainer OS include 28+ currencies to choose from, and the spreadsheets let you type your own currency code.
