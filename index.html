Questions

Good to know

Is this a subscription?

No. Every paid KREXZA tool is a one-time purchase. You pay once on Whop and download it straight away.

How do I get my tool after buying?

Checkout happens on Whop. Right after payment, your files are ready to download from your Whop account. Most tools take about two minutes to set up, and the apps and templates come with simple instructions.

Does it work on my iPhone?

The spreadsheet templates and PDF guides work on iPhone and iPad. The offline apps (Student OS, Freelancer OS, Trainer OS and the AI Prompt Vault) need a computer or an Android phone with Chrome. See the device table above.

Where is my data stored?

On your own device. The apps save to your browser, and the templates live in your own Google Drive or Excel file. KREXZA never sees your data. The apps include a backup button so you can move your data to another computer.

Do I need to know Excel or coding?

No. You type in the highlighted boxes and everything else calculates itself. The dashboard apps can load sample data so you can see how they work before adding your own.

Can I use these for my business?

Yes. Each license covers your own personal or business use. Please don't resell or share the files.
