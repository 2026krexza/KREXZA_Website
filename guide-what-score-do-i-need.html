What score do I needon my final exam?

The short answer: needed score = (target grade − current grade × (1 − final weight)) ÷ final weight. Here is how to use it.

The formula

Write the final exam's weight as a decimal. If the final is worth 40% of your grade, the weight is 0.4. Then: needed score = (target − current × (1 − weight)) ÷ weight.

Example 1: aiming for a B

Your current grade is 78%, the final is worth 40%, and you want 80% overall. Needed = (80 − 78 × 0.6) ÷ 0.4 = (80 − 46.8) ÷ 0.4 = 83%. You need 83% on the final.

Example 2: is an A still possible?

Your current grade is 85%, the final is worth 25%, and you want 90%. Needed = (90 − 85 × 0.75) ÷ 0.25 = (90 − 63.75) ÷ 0.25 = 105%. Above 100% means the target is out of reach, so aim for the next grade down and plan your time.

Turn the number into a study plan

  • Count the days left before the exam.
  • List the topics and mark the ones you are weakest in.
  • Study in focused 25 to 45 minute blocks, and keep a streak going.

Quick answers

Common questions.

How do I calculate the grade I need on a final exam?

Needed score = (target grade − current grade × (1 − final weight)) ÷ final weight, with the weight written as a decimal.

What if the score I need is over 100%?

The target is not reachable with the final alone. Aim for the next grade down, or ask whether extra credit is available.
