About KREXZA

Built in silent nights,made for big dreams.

KREXZA is a small studio of digital tools, designed and built by one self-taught web developer.

KREXZA started as late-night projects. Each tool began as a problem I or someone close to me had: a student juggling deadlines, a freelancer waiting on payment, a trainer tracking clients on paper.

Every tool is tested before it reaches the store. The formulas are checked, the apps are clicked through on desktop and phone, and the guides are proofread. If something doesn't work for you, tell me and I'll fix it.

No subscriptions, no accounts and no tracking inside the tools. You pay once, and your data stays with you.
