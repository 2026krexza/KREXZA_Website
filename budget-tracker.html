Questions

About Budget Tracker

Does the budget tracker connect to my bank?

No. You type in your own entries, which keeps your data private and works with any bank or currency.

Does it work on iPhone?

Yes. It works in the Google Sheets app on iPhone, iPad and Android, and in Excel or Google Sheets on a computer.

What is the 50/30/20 plan?

It splits income into 50% needs, 30% wants and 20% savings or debt payoff. The tracker shows how your real spending compares.
