Refund policy.

Every KREXZA tool is tested before release. If something doesn't work for you, we fix it first, and refund when we can't.

Digital products

KREXZA sells digital downloads: offline browser apps, spreadsheet templates and PDF guides. Because a digital file can't be returned once it has been downloaded, all sales are final, except in the cases below.

When you can get a refund

  • The file is broken, corrupted or will not open, and we can't fix it within 7 days.
  • The product is clearly different from its description on the product page.
  • You were charged twice for the same product by mistake. Duplicate charges are always refunded in full.

When a refund is not given

  • You changed your mind after downloading.
  • The device was not supported, when the product page clearly listed the supported devices (for example, an offline app on iPhone).
  • You no longer need the product, or bought it by mistake and already downloaded it.

How to ask for help

Message KREXZA through the store on Whop within 14 days of your purchase. Tell us the product name, what you expected and what happened, with a screenshot if possible. We reply within 2 business days, and most problems are fixed the same day.

How refunds are paid

Approved refunds are 90% of the amount you paid for that product. The remaining 10% covers payment processing and platform fees, which are not returned to KREXZA when a payment is refunded. Refunds are sent to your original payment method through Whop, and the time they take to appear depends on your bank or card provider.

Your rights under the consumer law of your country are not affected by this policy.
